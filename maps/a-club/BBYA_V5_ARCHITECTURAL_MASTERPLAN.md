# BBYA SOCIAL HUB — V5 ARCHITECTURAL MASTERPLAN

Status: **PHASE A — ARCHITECTURE / CIRCULATION ONLY**

This document is the spatial source-of-truth for BBYA V5. Decorative work must not change circulation widths, structural levels, major room boundaries, stair cores, lift core, or arrival sightlines without a deliberate architecture revision.

## 1. Master Envelope

- Building footprint: **180 studs wide × 220 studs deep**.
- X limits: **-90 to +90**.
- Z limits: **-90 rear to +130 front façade**.
- Exterior spawn/plaza sits beyond the façade on the +Z side.
- Structural levels:
  - Ground: **Y = 0**
  - VIP: **Y = 18**
  - Rooftop: **Y = 36**
- Typical floor-to-floor height: **18 studs**.
- Minimum public circulation width: **14 studs**.
- Main entrance clear opening: **40 studs**.
- Lobby-to-main-club clear opening: **40 studs**.
- Bar / Chill public doorway: **20 studs** from Main Club.
- Lobby side-room doorway: **14 studs**.
- Stair clear width: **9 studs per flight**.

## 2. Arrival Sequence

**Spawn → Exterior Plaza → BBYA Façade → Main Entrance → Lobby → Main Club**

Rules:
- Spawn must face the exterior façade.
- BBYA / SOCIAL HUB identity belongs on the **outside face** of the façade.
- No furniture, plants, sign blades, or decorative objects are allowed on the central approach axis.
- Entrance and lobby centerline remain visually clear.

## 3. Ground Floor Zoning

### Lobby
- Central public lobby approximately **120 × 42 studs**.
- Main axis kept empty for orientation.
- Left service band reserved for **restroom / service**.
- Right service band contains the **main lift core and lift waiting foyer**.

### Main Club
- Central hall bounded approximately by X **-54 to +54** and Z **-62 to +84**.
- Primary dance/crowd area remains central.
- DJ/stage is integrated into the rear building zone; it does not float in the room.
- Minimum side circulation around the stage: approximately **14 studs**.

### Bar — West
- Dedicated room approximately **32 × 50 studs**.
- Two logical access routes:
  - Lobby/front access.
  - Main Club side access.
- Must remain separate from the dance-floor circulation path.

### Chill Lounge — East
- Dedicated room approximately **32 × 50 studs**.
- Acoustically/spatially separated from the dance hall.
- Two access routes:
  - Lobby/front access.
  - Main Club side access.
- Chill furniture may not spill into the Main Club doorway.

### Back-of-House
- Rear-left and rear-right areas beside the stage are reserved for service/backstage functions.
- Public decorative placement must not consume these zones.

## 4. Vertical Circulation

### Main Lift
- Dedicated core in the east lobby/service band.
- Approximate shaft/core: **16 × 16 studs**.
- Stops / landings:
  - Ground
  - VIP
  - Rooftop
- Each level requires a clear waiting/landing area before users enter another programmed zone.

### Stairs
Two symmetrical stair cores:
- West stair core.
- East stair core.

Each floor transition uses a U-shaped stair:
- 2 flights.
- 12 risers per flight.
- 0.75 stud rise per riser.
- Total floor-to-floor rise: **18 studs**.
- Clear stair width: **9 studs**.
- Mid landing approximately **11 × 11 studs**.
- Stair core approx **25 × 38 studs**.

Stair access from Main Club occurs through dedicated **18-stud openings**, not through furniture or decorative gaps.

## 5. VIP Level

- West and East mezzanines align over the side service bands.
- Central void remains open above Main Club / dance floor.
- Front bridge creates a clear connection to the lift arrival.
- Rear bridge creates a circulation loop and supports the private zone.
- Inner mezzanine edges require architectural parapets/rails.

### Queen / Private Zone
- Reserved on the rear bridge.
- Approximately **42 × 16 studs** in the current greybox.
- Must remain separate from normal circulation.

### VIP Service
- West-front VIP zone reserved for service/restroom support.
- East-front VIP zone serves the lift arrival foyer.

## 6. Rooftop Masterplan

The rooftop is **not a neon extension of the club**. It is planned as a separate luxury-resort atmosphere.

### Rooftop Circulation
Before any furniture or landscaping is placed:
- Central north-south spine: **16 studs wide**.
- Stair cross-axis: **14 studs wide**.
- Lift cross-axis: **14 studs wide**.
- Rooftop arrival plaza: approximately **42 × 22 studs**.

No pool, bar, cabana, sofa, plant, or photo prop may block these axes.

### Pool Zone
- Central/rear position.
- Current program footprint: **76 × 42 studs**.
- Keep **12+ studs** of usable deck/circulation around major wet-zone edges before furniture placement.
- Future atmosphere: water/turquoise + warm indirect lighting, not full-club neon.

### Sky Bar
- West rooftop.
- Program footprint approximately **40 × 34 studs**.
- Close enough to pool/social deck for natural flow, but outside the wet circulation path.

### Rooftop Chill
- East rooftop.
- Program footprint approximately **40 × 34 studs**.
- Lower-energy lounge identity than Main Club.

### Cabana Program Zones
- West and East flanking the pool.
- Approximate current zone per side: **30 × 24 studs**.
- Cabana objects must remain inside these future program rectangles.

### Sunset / Social Deck
- Central front/mid rooftop.
- Approximate current zone: **48 × 28 studs**.

### Photo / View Deck
- Front view edge of rooftop.
- Approximate current zone: **48 × 18 studs**.
- Must not become a mandatory circulation choke point.

## 7. Atmosphere Lock for Future Phases

### Main Club
Allowed future direction:
- high-energy neon
- dark material palette
- pink / cyan / purple lighting
- dense nightlife atmosphere

### Rooftop
Allowed future direction:
- tropical luxury / resort
- warm ambient lighting
- natural stone / wood / glass character
- turquoise pool lighting
- restrained BBYA pink/cyan accents only

Do **not** make the rooftop another full-neon dance room.

## 8. Architecture Freeze Rule

Before Phase B (materials/lighting) starts, playtest must confirm:
1. Main entrance is immediately obvious from spawn.
2. Lobby is readable without signage overload.
3. Main Club doorway is obvious.
4. Bar and Chill are clearly separate destinations.
5. Both stair cores are easy to find and walk without snagging.
6. Lift location makes spatial sense on all three levels.
7. VIP circulation forms a coherent loop.
8. Rooftop arrival provides a clear orientation point before pool/bar/chill zones.
9. No circulation route depends on teleporting into furniture.
10. No decorative asset is needed to understand the building.

Only after these pass may Phase B and Phase C begin.
