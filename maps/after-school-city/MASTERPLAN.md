# AFTER SCHOOL CITY — MASTERPLAN v1.0

## Core vision
A compact school-life roleplay/simulation where the playable fantasy begins after the final school bell. Players choose activities, explore, earn, customize and return for new goals/events.

Core loop:

`School Out → Explore → Choose Activity → Play → Earn XP/Coins → Customize → Meet Friends → Event → Return`

## Audience / content direction
- Primary audience: early teens / middle-school transition.
- Stylish and youthful, not toddler-like and not adult nightlife.
- Friendship, hobbies, exploration and self-expression.
- Social interaction supports gameplay but is not the sole purpose of the experience.

## District plan
### School District
Spawn, courtyard, cafeteria, club rooms, music/art rooms and first onboarding activity.

### Downtown
Arcade, cafe, convenience shop, fashion storefront, photo booth/plaza and future rotating shop.

### Skate Park
Fast repeatable skill activity, trick challenges and short competitions.

### City Park
Green space, lake, photo hunt, treasure hunt and seasonal event routes.

### Residential
Gateway to future player bedroom/studio-room customization.

### Sports Field
Basketball/futsal timed challenges, scoreboards and weekend tournaments.

## Progression
Working name: `Life Level`.

Suggested milestone labels:
- Level 1: New Kid
- Level 5: Explorer
- Level 10: Regular
- Level 20: City Insider
- Level 30: Local Legend

Progression must be earned primarily through play, not Robux purchases.

## Activity roadmap
First playable set:
1. Basketball Shootout.
2. Skate Trick Challenge.
3. Arcade Timing Game.

Expansion set:
- Rhythm challenge.
- Photo Hunt.
- Bike/Delivery Dash.
- Hide & Seek event.
- City Treasure Hunt.

Target activity duration: roughly 30 seconds to 3 minutes.

## Clubs
Future clubs: Gaming, Music, Sports, Art, Skate, Photography.
Clubs should grant missions, XP, badges and cosmetics without permanently locking the player to one identity.

## Personalization
- Avatar/outfit styling.
- Backpack/accessories.
- Skateboard/bike/scooter cosmetics.
- Emotes and poses.
- Personal bedroom/studio-room furniture.

## UI direction
Clean mobile-first HUD with a phone shell for Map, Missions, Club, Events, Wardrobe, Friends and Camera.

## Retention
- Daily missions.
- Weekly missions.
- Unlock milestones.
- Rotating city events.
- Seasonal cosmetics and collectibles.

## Safety / maturity guard
Do not design the core experience around dating, romance, gambling, alcohol, realistic weapons, sexualized cosmetics or adult nightlife. Free-form social interaction is supportive, while activities, roles, quests and progression remain the game identity.

## Monetization direction
Cosmetics/personalization only for the first monetization phase: room themes, vehicle skins, emotes, outfit cosmetics and optional premium spaces. Avoid pay-to-win progression.

## Production phases
### Phase 0 — Blockout
Validate city scale, district placement and traversal.

### Phase 1 — Playable MVP
School + Downtown + Skate Park, 3 mini-games, coins, XP, daily missions and basic shop.

### Phase 2 — Social systems
Clubs, group emotes, photo mode, events and friend-friendly spawning.

### Phase 3 — Personalization
Bedroom, furniture, rides and expanded fashion.

### Phase 4 — Retention
Weekly quests, achievements, collectibles and seasonal progression.

### Phase 5 — Live operations
Regular events, map additions, cosmetics and new activities.
