# Mountain Social Adventure — Master Lock v1.0

Status: LOCKED
Owner: ACC / CREATIVE LAB

## Core identity
Original/fictitious ACC mountain world. The experience is NOT a generic obby and must not become a copy of a real-world mountain map.

Primary experience pillars:
1. Hiking journey
2. Exploration
3. Social hangout
4. Cinematic ambience
5. Secrets and replay discovery

## World journey
Basecamp → lower forest → river/waterfall → ridge → mist forest → upper camp → rocky climb → sea of clouds → summit → secret route/secret summit.

## Required systems
- Mobile/HP friendly
- Checkpoint + save-ready structure
- Dynamic day/night foundation
- Sunrise/sunset ambience
- Fog/rain/weather foundation
- Campfire/camping zones
- Social/carry-ready interaction anchors
- Photo spots
- Summit counter/leaderboard/title-ready anchors
- Hidden trail + secret summit
- Modular and reusable systems
- Low-maintenance / automation-ready architecture

## Hard constraints
- Do not turn the map into a rectangular obstacle course.
- Do not use one ambience from bottom to summit.
- Checkpoints must represent memorable locations, not only numbers.
- Summit must be a destination/hangout/payoff, not an instant teleport-out trigger.
- Do not modify or publish BBYA Social Hub, WONDERPOCKET, Bali Virtual Nightlife, or any other map while developing this project.
- Publishing target must remain disabled until Mountain has its own verified Universe ID and Place ID.
